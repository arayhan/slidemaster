# SlideMaster — Lessons

Rules this project learned the hard way. Each entry: the date, what happened, and the rule derived from it. A lesson without a rule is an anecdote — always extract the rule.

## 2000-01-01 — Reviewers reviewing their own designs

**What happened:** A reviewer approved work built on a design they had authored themselves; the design's blind spot sailed through untouched, because the person checking the work shared the assumption that caused the flaw.

**Rule:** A reviewer never reviews a design they authored. Whoever designs a piece of work hands review of it to someone (or some agent) who had no part in the design — fresh eyes are the entire point of review.
